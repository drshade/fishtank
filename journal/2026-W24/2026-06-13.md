# 2026-06-13

## First full water test panel — and the soft-water story was wrong

Colombo Aqua triple kit arrived; tested with it plus the Tetra kit.

**Nitrogen (all zero, all good):**
- Ammonia 0.0 mg/L
- Nitrite 0.0 mg/L
- Nitrate 0.0 mg/L — normal for a heavily-planted, wildly-understocked
  tank; plants consume nitrate as fast as it's produced. Not a miscycle.

**Hardness / pH — the surprise:**
- GH **~2 °dH** (very soft / calcium-poor). First test "never changed
  colour"; retest showed a brief green flash at drop 2 then yellow for
  the remaining ~18 drops. That green flash *is* the titration endpoint —
  GH 2. Two attempts agreed.
- KH **11 °dH** (high — carbonate-rich)
- pH **7.5** (alkaline) — confirms KH 11 is real, not a misread.

**What this overturns:** the tank has been run on a "soft, acidic
Amazonia water" assumption (target KH 2-4, pH lowered by soil). The
measurements say the opposite: **calcium-poor but carbonate-rich and
alkaline.** The ADA Amazonia is ~7 months old; its acid/KH buffering is
spent (fresh Amazonia holds pH ~6.5, strips KH toward 0). It still pulls
Ca/Mg out via cation exchange, so GH stays low — while carbonate from
Amsterdam tap top-ups accumulates, driving KH and pH up. Classic
aged-active-substrate drift.

**Consequences:**
- **Nerite shell erosion re-diagnosed.** Not acid dissolution (water's
  alkaline) — it's **calcium starvation** (GH 2). Nerites are
  calcium-hungry and show the deficit first; the neos cope (three
  successful broods), so the snails are the canary. Same fix direction
  (add calcium) but a different mechanism and a different product.
- **Remineraliser: APT Sky is the right tool, mislabelled.** It's a
  **GH-only** booster (Ca 21.5% + Mg 5.4%, leaves KH and pH unchanged) —
  exactly what GH 2 / KH 11 needs. The repo had it filed as "GH/KH+";
  corrected. A GH/KH+ product would have pushed the already-high KH
  higher. Dose slowly (babies present). Note: APT Sky has trace copper —
  dose conservatively, never overdose.
- **Cuttlebone ruled out.** It's calcium *carbonate* — would raise the
  already-high KH, and barely dissolves at pH 7.5. APT Sky covers the
  calcium need without the carbonate. Dropped from the shopping list.
- **Don't chase KH down.** 11 is fine for neos and stable; messing with
  stable water to hit a textbook number is the wrong trade with broods
  in the tank.

**Ammonia gate for Dennerle removal: precondition met** (NH3 0 / NO2 0).
But that's with both filters running — proves the precondition, not that
the canister alone holds the load. Brood 2 is only ~8 days old, so don't
pull the Dennerle yet; wait until ~2026-06-19, then remove and test
ammonia daily for 3-4 days.
