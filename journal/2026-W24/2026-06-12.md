# 2026-06-12

## Pi Zero 2 W won — tank telemetry project planned

Tom won a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W (during the week of 1-7 June 2026).
Decided to point it at the tank as a **read-only telemetry node** — it
observes and logs, it never actuates. Smart plugs keep all control;
a Pi that only watches can't kill shrimp.

Two phases:

1. **Temp logging** — DS18B20 waterproof probe on GPIO, continuous log,
   phone alert if out of the 22-24°C band. First real job: properly
   verify the canister thermo's 24h hold (currently gated on squinting
   at the uncalibrated mercury thermometer).
2. **Camera** — NoIR module + IR illuminator for pre-dawn snapshots.
   Targets the one observation the current regime can't make: whether
   the adults are surface-hanging at ~4am (the O2 near-miss window,
   see 2026-W21/2026-05-22.md). Plus remote tank checks on trips and
   a colony-growth timelapse.

Scope expanded same day with two more (same Pi, same read-only
principle):

3. **Leak sensor** — moisture probe on the cabinet floor under the
   canister, push alert. The canister sits below the waterline with
   ~2m of hose = siphon-the-tank-onto-the-floor potential; new failure
   mode taken on 2026-05-26, currently unmonitored.
4. **E-ink status display** — temp, last measurement, days since water
   change, next date-gated task, pulled from this repo. Zero light
   emission (matters next to the photoperiod), purely for delight.
5. **Ambient temp/humidity sensor (BME280)** — logs room conditions
   alongside water temp. Water-vs-ambient delta quantifies what the
   desk fan actually buys during heatwaves; RH predicts whether
   evaporative cooling will work (37% RH in May was ideal — won't
   always hold) and how fast top-ups will be needed.

Ruled out: auto-feeder / auto-doser / full controller builds — they fix
non-problems on a stable 17L nano and add failure modes. Camera-based
shrimp counting noted as a stretch goal only — CV on translucent
shrimplets in dense hairgrass is unlikely to be census-grade.

## 3D printer joins the project

Tom also has a 3D printer. Print list added to the telemetry project
(camera bracket, e-ink enclosure, fan mount, Pi case, leak-sensor
holder, clips) — all dry-side. Settled a filament rule before the
temptation arises: prints stay out of the water by default; anything
submerged must be plain uncoloured PETG, never metallic/silk/glitter/
glow filament (pigment additives can contain copper — the neo killer).

## Parts list for the build (phases 1-4)

**Phase 1+3 — sensors (~€20-30):**
- DS18B20 waterproof probe, stainless, 1m+ cable (~€4)
- 4.7kΩ resistor (~€1, or assortment pack)
- BME280 breakout, I2C 3.3V (~€7) — beware €3 "BME280" listings that
  ship BMP280 clones: no humidity sensor. Buy from a seller that
  states Bosch BME280 explicitly.
- Water-leak / moisture sensor module (~€2)
- 2×20 GPIO header for the Zero 2 W (~€2) — it ships unpopulated;
  solder or use a solderless hammer header
- Female-female Dupont jumper wires (~€3)
- MicroSD 32GB A1 + any 5V micro-USB PSU (likely already on hand)

**Power-loss watchdog (optional, ~€20):** powerbank with pass-through
charging, or a Waveshare UPS HAT (C). Most cheap powerbanks cut output
momentarily when mains returns — that reboots the Pi and defeats the
purpose. UPS HAT is the clean answer.

**Phase 2 — camera (~€50):**
- Raspberry Pi Camera Module 3 NoIR (~€40) — autofocus + good low light
- Zero-size CSI ribbon, 22-pin to 15-pin (~€4) — the Zero's camera
  connector is smaller than full-size Pis; the standard cable won't fit
- 850nm IR illuminator board (~€10)

**Phase 4 — display (~€20):**
- Waveshare 2.13" e-Paper HAT (Zero footprint, sits directly on the
  header)

NL sources: Kiwi Electronics (official RPi reseller) for camera/HAT,
TinyTronics for the cheap sensor bits. All-in ~€90-110; sensors-only
start ~€20.
