# 2026-06-12

## Pi Zero 2 W won — tank telemetry project planned

Tom won a Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W (during the week of 1-7 June 2026).
Decided to point it at the tank as a **read-only telemetry node** — it
observes and logs, it never actuates. Smart plugs keep all control;
a Pi that only watches can't kill shrimp.

Two phases:

1. **Temp logging** — DS18B20 waterproof probe on GPIO, continuous log,
   phone alert if out of the 22-24°C band. First real job: properly
   verify the canister thermo's 24h hold (currently gated on squinting
   at the uncalibrated mercury thermometer).
2. **Camera** — NoIR module + IR illuminator for pre-dawn snapshots.
   Targets the one observation the current regime can't make: whether
   the adults are surface-hanging at ~4am (the O2 near-miss window,
   see 2026-W21/2026-05-22.md). Plus remote tank checks on trips and
   a colony-growth timelapse.

Scope expanded same day with two more (same Pi, same read-only
principle):

3. **Leak sensor** — moisture probe on the cabinet floor under the
   canister, push alert. The canister sits below the waterline with
   ~2m of hose = siphon-the-tank-onto-the-floor potential; new failure
   mode taken on 2026-05-26, currently unmonitored.
4. **E-ink status display** — temp, last measurement, days since water
   change, next date-gated task, pulled from this repo. Zero light
   emission (matters next to the photoperiod), purely for delight.

Ruled out: auto-feeder / auto-doser / full controller builds — they fix
non-problems on a stable 17L nano and add failure modes. Camera-based
shrimp counting noted as a stretch goal only — CV on translucent
shrimplets in dense hairgrass is unlikely to be census-grade.
