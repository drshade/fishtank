# 2026-05-25

## Mystery critters ID'd: ostracods

![Ostracods on the glass — tiny cream-coloured oval/seed shapes](2026-05-25-ostracods-on-glass.jpg)

Tom asked about tiny "stick insect looking" glass-clear critters all
over the tank — Michelle's brother had said they're a sign of a
healthy tank.

Photo settles it: **ostracods** (Ostracoda, "seed shrimp"). Not
stick-like on closer look — they're 1-2mm oval/sesame-seed shapes
with a hinged bivalve-like carapace, crawling on the glass. Cream
to whitish.

- Harmless detritivores — eat biofilm, decaying organics, leftover food
- Free shrimplet snack
- Reproduce by parthenogenesis → population booms with surplus food →
  useful overfeeding indicator
- Their presence = mature, stable biofilm. Brother-in-law was right.

Not hydra, not planaria, not damselfly nymphs. Nothing that touches
shrimplets. Stand down.

## Water source confirmed: plain Amsterdam tap, no dechlorinator

Tom noted his lifelong practice: top up and change with **tap water
direct** (temperature-matched, introduced slowly via a small tube over
~20-30 min, historically ~50% volume), never dechlorinated — no trouble,
in Amsterdam.

Confirmed correct, and *why*: the Netherlands (Waternet) distributes
drinking water with **no chlorine or chloramine residual** —
biostability via treatment (dune infiltration, ozonation, GAC, slow sand
filtration), not a disinfectant. Nothing to gas off or neutralise. A
dechlorinator solves a non-problem here. Settled.

Caveats surfaced:
- **Copper, not chlorine, is the plumbing risk** for neos. Run the cold
  tap until properly cold before collecting (draws from the main, not
  overnight pipe-sat water).
- **Volume is the live risk, not the source.** ~50% was fine pre-babies;
  while brood 1 is out and brood 2 is due mid-June, hold to the 10-15%
  protocol. The slow 20-30 min introduction is good practice — keep it.
- **Tap acts as a mild remineraliser** (~GH 8) against the
  KH/GH-stripping aquasoil — part of why the nerites lasted before shell
  erosion. Don't switch to pure RO without remineralising; Salty Shrimp
  GH/KH+ (already on the list) remains the right buy.

moot given the above (she's also an NL keeper); left as-is, append-only.
