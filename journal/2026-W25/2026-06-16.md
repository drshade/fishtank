# 2026-06-16

## First GH dose — 1g APT GH booster

Dosed **1g of APT GH** (gypsum-based, calcium sulfate) into ~17L — first
remineraliser dose. Made it up as a slurry in tank water and tipped the
whole thing into the outflow.

- **Confirmed gypsum**: the stock wouldn't go clear (CaSO4 solubility
  ~2.4 g/L), which also confirms it's genuinely GH-only — Ca + sulfate, no
  carbonate, so it won't touch KH. Cloudy cup is a small-container problem;
  17L dissolves it fine over a few hours.
- **Method learned**: slurry settles too fast in the jug to dose evenly —
  next time dose the weighed powder **dry, straight into the strong outflow
  current** (away from the carpet/babies). Tom's scale is accurate to 0.1g,
  so weighing the gram is easy; the slurry step was the only awkward bit.
- **Rough expectation**: ~+2 dGH for 1g in 17L if mostly gypsum → maybe
  GH ~3 tomorrow off a post-water-change baseline of ~1. Guess off an
  assumed maker target — **the GH retest tomorrow morning is the real
  number** and calibrates exact grams-per-dGH for the next step.
- Reminder: water changes dilute GH (tap is GH 0), so re-dose after changes.
- **TDS after dosing: 400 → 440 immediately → 462 by +2hrs** as the haze
  dissolved (+62 from 1g, near-settled). Confirms the dose landed. Live demo
  of the tap coupling: the water change dropped TDS, this dose puts ~62 back.
