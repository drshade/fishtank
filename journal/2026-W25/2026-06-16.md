## First GH dose — 1g APT GH booster

Dosed **1g of APT GH** (gypsum-based, calcium sulfate) into ~17L — first
remineraliser dose. Made it up as a slurry in tank water and tipped the
whole thing into the outflow.

- **Confirmed gypsum**: the stock wouldn't go clear (CaSO4 solubility
  ~2.4 g/L), which also confirms it's genuinely GH-only — Ca + sulfate, no
  carbonate, so it won't touch KH. Cloudy cup is a small-container problem;
  17L dissolves it fine over a few hours.
- **Method learned**: slurry settles too fast in the jug to dose evenly —
  next time dose the weighed powder **dry, straight into the strong outflow
  current** (away from the carpet/babies). Tom's scale is accurate to 0.1g,
  so weighing the gram is easy; the slurry step was the only awkward bit.
- **Rough expectation**: ~+2 dGH for 1g in 17L if mostly gypsum → maybe
  GH ~3 tomorrow off a post-water-change baseline of ~1. Guess off an
  assumed maker target — **the GH retest tomorrow morning is the real
  number** and calibrates exact grams-per-dGH for the next step.
- Reminder: water changes dilute GH (tap is GH 0), so re-dose after changes.
- **TDS after dosing: 400 → 440 immediately → 462 by +2hrs** as the haze
  dissolved (+62 from 1g, near-settled). Confirms the dose landed. Live demo
  of the tap coupling: the water change dropped TDS, this dose puts ~62 back.

## GH test method error — both GH readings VOID

Tom caught it: the GH test starts yellow and you titrate until the **whole
vial holds green** — the transient green *flash* where a drop lands is NOT
the endpoint. Earlier readings (and I, this session) treated the flash as
done. So both GH numbers are wrong and **understated**:

- Tank "GH 2" (2026-06-13) and tap "GH 0" (2026-06-15) are **void** — real
  GH is higher for both, unknown until a proper retest.
- This **suspends a whole chain of conclusions** I'd built on them today:
  low GH / "dosing is the only calcium lever" / tap-GH-0 / "soil-stripping
  was wrong" / RO reframe / and the nerite erosion as calcium *starvation*.
  All pending a correct GH retest. I stacked too much on an unverified
  method — twice today. Owned.
- **Still solid** (different tests, unaffected): TDS (tap 274, tank
  494→400→462) and KH (tank 11, tap 7). Worth checking KH wasn't read the
  same flawed way.
- The 1g GH dose stands and is harmless either way; **no more dosing until
  GH is re-measured properly** — it may not even be needed.

**Action: retest GH on tank + tap — drops until the whole vial holds green,
count all.** That gives the first trustworthy GH number.

say it should go **red (GH present) → green (endpoint)**; Tom saw **no red
at all**, only fleeting green flashes where drops land. No red base colour =
the indicator dye has degraded. Kit's ~18 months old; the KH reagent from
the same kit still works (the GH dye is the fussier one and dies first).
