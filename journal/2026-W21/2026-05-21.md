# 2026-05-21

## Glass haze + biofilm fuzz on rocks
Both normal for a mature planted tank. Glass biofilm = shrimplet food.
Rock fuzz = early-stage soft algae, shrimp will graze it down. Not a
problem unless visually unacceptable.

Plan: small water change this week (10-15%, slow drip refill, matched
temp). Surface-siphon only over the hairgrass — do not penetrate the
carpet while babies are still tiny. Inspect siphon output in a clear
container for 10 min before disposing.

→ added to `memory/knowledge.md` (Water changes section, carpet rule)

## No babies visible all day
Expected — shrimplets hide in the hairgrass base for the first 1-3 weeks.
Will become reliably visible at ~5mm body length. Flashlight check after
dark recommended if reassurance wanted.

Not a cause for action.
