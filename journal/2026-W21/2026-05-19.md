# 2026-05-19

## Dennerle filter cleaned
Was clogged and visibly sluggish, hence weaker flow. Cleaned with media
squeezed in tank water (not tap). Flow restored. Noted: this is the
second clean in recent memory — bioload + plant detritus is hitting this
filter harder than it can handle long-term.

## Decision: upgrade to canister filter

After working through options, settled on:

- **Oase FiltoSmart Thermo 100** canister
- **GreenWorks 13mm nano lily pipes** (inflow + outflow) from greenaqua.hu
- **Stainless steel mesh filter guard** over inflow (shrimplet-safe,
  much cleaner aesthetic than foam pre-filter sponge)
- **Eheim Double Tap 12/16mm × 2** for click-disconnect maintenance
- Transparent hose for visible portion

Why FiltoSmart Thermo 100 specifically:
- Integrated heater → **remove the in-tank heater** (biggest aesthetic
  win, eliminates the ugliest piece of equipment)
- 12/16mm tubing → wide selection of lily pipes
- Rated to 100L but throttleable → headroom + future flexibility
- Right physical size for this tank

Ruled out:
- **BioMaster 2 Thermo 250** — physically bigger than the tank itself,
  comically oversized
- **Sponge filter as primary** — fights CO2 injection, aesthetic
  regression, still needs an air pump outside the tank
- **Mattenfilter** — overkill for this size
