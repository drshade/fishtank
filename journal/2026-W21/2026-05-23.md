# 2026-05-23

## AS25 bubbles too coarse

![AS25 producing 2-4mm bubbles, much coarser than ideal](2026-05-23-as25-bubbles.jpg)

Bubbles from the AS25 are 2-4mm at source — way bigger than ideal.
Compare to the Chihiros CO2 diffuser which produces ~0.1mm mist. Two
problems:

- **Noisy**: each coarse bubble pops audibly at the surface
- **Less efficient O2 transfer**: bigger bubbles rise faster, less
  surface area, less time in contact with water

Cause: the AS25 is on the coarse end of "fine pore" — Tetra's marketing
is generous. The Tetra AirSilent Mini also doesn't have the pressure
to drive a finer ceramic effectively (spare Chihiros CO2 diffuser
tested earlier — pump can't push it enough).

## Fix plan

1. **Throttle with a flow control valve** — next shop trip, €1-2. Less
   air through same stone → smaller bubbles + quieter. Inline between
   check valve and airstone.
2. If throttling alone isn't enough → upgrade to a sintered glass
   diffuser (~€8-10), looks cleaner anyway.

Living with the current setup for now. Tank doesn't care about bubble
size — this is purely a noise / aesthetics issue.
