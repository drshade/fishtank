### Genetics implication flagged

Clear × Green is cross-morph breeding. Selected Neocaridina colour
traits are polygenic, so F1 offspring usually revert to wild-type
translucent brown/grey rather than holding either parent's colour.

So: the current and future broods will probably **not** be uniformly
green or clear. Expect a mixed colony dominated by wild-type colours
over 3-6 months. Doesn't affect tank health — just sets expectations.

If a colour-consistent colony matters down the line, Tom would need
to start over with same-morph adults (e.g. all Green Jade) and cull
off-colours.
