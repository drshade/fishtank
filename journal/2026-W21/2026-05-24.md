## Implication for canister install

The original plan was "inflow in the dead corner, outflow opposite
side." With the corrected geometry, inflow goes to **front-right** —
which means the lily pipe + stainless mesh filter guard ends up at the
front of the tank, visible, rather than tucked at the back.

This is a real aesthetic-vs-functional trade-off worth deciding at
install time, not now:

- **Option A — inflow in front-right (original plan)**: actively pulls
  accumulated detritus out. Most effective. Lily pipe + guard visible
  from the front.
- **Option B — inflow at back, outflow blowing into front-right**:
  cleaner aesthetic. Less effective at removing *existing*
  accumulation, but should prevent new accumulation once flow reaches
  the corner. Probably fine after the initial mulm is cleared
  manually.

Both are defensible. Lean A if the detritus is bad; lean B if it
clears once flow gets there.
