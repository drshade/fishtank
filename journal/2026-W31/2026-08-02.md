# 2026-08-02

## Sword plant removed; shrimp parked in Michelle's tank

The big sword plant was overgrowing the tank — it had come to dominate the
20L cube (it already blocked most top-down views) — so it came out today.
Uprooting a root-feeder that size from mature Amazonia is a major
disturbance: substrate stirred, ammonia-spike risk for a few days.

Both founder adults (**Mommy** and **Dad**) were moved to **Michelle's
tank** ahead of the disruption. Plan: let this tank settle for a few days,
then move them back. **Test ammonia before returning them** — the uproot is
exactly the kind of event that puts a transient NH3 pulse through Amazonia.

The tank is shrimp-free for the first time since the colony started; current
occupants are the 2 nerites + microfauna.

## Brood 3 lost — never released

Brood 3 (berried confirmed 2026-06-19, release expected ~early July) **never
released — no babies ever appeared**. Confirmed lost as of today. That makes
**three consecutive failed broods** (brood 1 released ~05-16, gone by
mid-June; brood 2 released 06-05, gone by 06-28; brood 3 never released at
all). Colony growth so far: zero.

A dropped/failed clutch is a different failure mode than post-release
shrimplet loss — heat is the timeline suspect again (the late-June heatwave
had the tank at 27.7°C while she was carrying), and the still-unknown GH
remains a live suspect for both molt quality and egg viability. **The GH
retest is now the single most load-bearing open item** if breeding is ever
going to work in this water.

## Decision: display tank, not breeding colony

Settled later the same day: **this is a display tank now.** The breeding
project is shelved — 0/3 broods despite visibly healthy, repeatedly-berried
adults, and the *why* was never pinned down (heat and unknown GH were the
suspects). Meanwhile **Michelle's tank is producing lots of babies that
survive well**, so shrimp supply is solved externally: top up this tank
with teenagers from her colony whenever numbers thin.

Consequences:

- **Betta: greenlit in principle** (was "considering" this morning). With
  breeding abandoned, the shrimplet-predation objection stops binding —
  attrition on shrimp is acceptable and replaceable from Michelle's stock.
  Selection/prep checklist now in `pending.md`.
- **GH retest demoted** from "most load-bearing open item" to
  nice-to-have — it was load-bearing *for breeding*. Still mildly
  interesting for the nerite shell question.
- **QT tank + genetics-refresh + Marktplaats plans dropped** — all were
  predicated on a growing colony. Michelle's tank is the same household
  water and disease pool; her teenagers don't need a 6-12-month QT
  pipeline.
- Shrimplet-protection rules (carpet siphon bans, baby-gated water
  changes) become conditional history — they only reactivate if a berried
  female ever matters again.
