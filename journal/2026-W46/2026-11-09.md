# 2026-11-09

## Pi camera back up — mount design started

The camera Pi (`tankspy`) was powered back up after sitting idle since the
aiming/focus session on 2026-07-05. Camera and `tools/grab-frame.sh` still
work; nothing is automated on the Pi yet (no cron, no trigger). The missing
piece is a mount, so that came first: Tom is modelling it in Onshape and
printing on the Bambu A1 mini in PLA.

**First attempt — stacked case, dropped.** A 75×36mm tray for the Pi with
the camera hanging under a lid. Two problems: the camera and its pins land
on the Pi's factory-soldered GPIO header, and the whole thing comes out
~27mm thick with the lens poking out.

**Direction settled — thin hanging tray.** Pi and camera side by side on
one base (~15mm thick), hung flat against the glass from a gravity hook
over the rim, Pi on top, camera at the bottom and upside down (fixed with
`--rotation 180`). The lens looks through the tray floor, recessed ~1mm
with a flared opening, so the flat face of the case sits on the glass and
no separate shroud is needed. Neck length between hook and case sets the
framing; Tom's calc puts the lens at 11cm below the rim minimum, and
~14-15cm is about mid water column.

**Pin fit test.** Two test plates: pins at the nominal hole size locate
the boards without binding — camera Ø2.2mm (2.25 too tight), Pi Ø2.75mm
(2.8 doesn't fit). A true friction fit sits inside a 0.05mm window, tighter
than print-to-print repeatability, so the pins only locate; how the boards
are held down (lid posts or snap hooks) is still open.

**Test frames (hand-held against the glass, in `tools/frames/`, not kept).**
First frames were soft at every focus setting: a smudged lens plus hand
shake at the automatic 1/17s shutter. After a wipe and a forced 1/160s
shutter the frame was sharp. Lessons: the lens must never touch the glass
(it blocks the focus mechanism — hence the recess), a fixed mount removes
the shake, and the view runs straight through the tank to the room behind.

## Flat tray v1 reviewed; hold-down settled

First export of the side-by-side tray (`flat mount`, 130×36×15.5mm)
checked against the drawings: both pin patterns exact, USB slot centred
on the power port, lens window on the right hole pair. Fixes from the
review: camera posts 9→8mm (the lens tip had been 2.2mm behind the outer
face, enough for the 102° edge rays to clip an 11mm straight window) plus
a 45° chamfer on the outside of the window; USB slot to widen 12.5→14mm.
I wrongly read the camera drawing with the connector on the wrong edge
and predicted a ribbon U-turn — Tom checked the real board: the lens is
level with the pair nearer the connector, so the ribbon runs straight to
the Pi and the hanging image is upside down (`--rotation 180` stands).

**Hold-down decided**: the lid carries Ø5/Ø3 tubes that drop over each
locating pin onto the board, and is itself located by four corner L-tabs
(0.1-0.15mm offset) rather than a full-perimeter lip. The hook/neck will
be a separate part slotted into the top end — the over-rim leg points at
the bed when the tray prints flat, and a separate neck makes the drop
tunable.

→ updated memory/pending.md (Mount: hold-down, tray v1 notes)
